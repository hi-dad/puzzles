Poozles, on the web and on iPhone

Something not working, or an idea? Email daniel.ryan312@gmail.com and I'll get back to you.

My puzzles aren't showing

Puzzles belong to the account you signed in with. Sign in the same way on every device: the same Google account, or Apple with the same Apple ID. If you sign in with Apple, choose Share My Email so your puzzles find you on the website too.

Playing together

Open a puzzle, open its menu and tap Play together to send a link or a 6-letter code. Up to 4 people can play at once, and everyone needs to sign in. Only people you invite can see a shared puzzle.

Leaving a shared puzzle

Delete it from your home screen. It disappears for you only; the others keep playing.

Reporting a problem with someone

If someone you played with shared something inappropriate, delete the puzzle to leave it, and email daniel.ryan312@gmail.com with the puzzle's invite code. It will be looked at and removed if needed.

Deleting your account

Tap the person icon at the top right of the home screen and choose Delete account. Your email, name, photos and puzzles are erased from the server straight away.
