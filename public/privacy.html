Privacy

Poozles, on the web and on iPhone · Last updated October 29, 2026

Poozles turns your photos into jigsaw puzzles. It collects as little as it can, never sells anything, and has no ads or tracking.

What Poozles stores

Without signing in, everything stays on your device: your photos, puzzles and progress never leave it.

If you sign in with Apple or Google, Poozles receives your email address and first name from them and uses them only to know which puzzles are yours and to show your name to people you play with.

Once you're signed in, Poozles keeps a copy of your puzzle photos and your progress on its server, so they appear on every device you sign in on.

Where it is kept

Puzzles and photos are stored with Cloudflare, which runs the Poozles server. They are private to your account. If you invite people to play a puzzle together, they can see that puzzle's photo and your first name, and nothing else of yours. You can only be added to a shared puzzle by opening someone's invite.

What Poozles does not do

No ads, no analytics or tracking, no selling or sharing your data, and no access to any photos except the ones you pick for a puzzle.

Deleting your data

Tap the person icon at the top right of the home screen and choose Delete account. This erases your email, name, photos and puzzles from the server straight away, and removes you from puzzles you were playing with others. A puzzle you shared stays with the people still playing it until the last of them deletes it.
