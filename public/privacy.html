Privacy

Poozles, on the web and on iPhone · Last updated October 27, 2026

Poozles turns your photos into jigsaw puzzles. It collects as little as it can, never sells anything, and has no ads or tracking.

What Poozles stores

Without signing in, everything stays on your device: your photos, puzzles and progress never leave it.

If you sign in with Apple or Google, Poozles receives your email address from them and uses it only to know which puzzles are yours. It then keeps a copy of your puzzle photos and your progress on its server, so they appear on every device you sign in on.

Where it is kept

Synced puzzles and photos are stored with Cloudflare, which runs the Poozles server. They are private to your account and not shared with anyone else.

What Poozles does not do

No ads, no analytics or tracking, no selling or sharing your data, and no access to any photos except the ones you pick for a puzzle.

Deleting your data

Tap your account (the “Synced” badge on the home screen) and choose Delete account. This erases your email, photos and puzzles from the server straight away. Puzzles on a device you never signed in on stay only on that device and go away when you remove the app or clear the website's data.
