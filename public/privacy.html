Privacy

Last updated October 31, 2026

Without signing in, your photos and puzzles stay on your device.

If you sign in with Apple or Google, Poozles receives your email address and first name, and stores your puzzle photos and progress with Cloudflare so they sync across your devices. People you invite to a puzzle can see its photo and your first name.

Poozles has no ads or tracking and doesn't sell your data.

To erase your data, tap the person icon at the top right of the home screen and choose Delete account. A puzzle you shared stays with the others still playing it.
